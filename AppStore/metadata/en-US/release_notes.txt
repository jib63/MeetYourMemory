New in 1.1.0: more room, more ways to play!

• 14 new memory games: 10 for a partially folded iPhone Duo and 4 for a fully unfolded Duo or iPad, including Panorama Pairs, Orbit Map, Sound Board and Comet Field.
• Games adapt to your screen. Start your usual scan and the next challenge is chosen from the games available for your current display.
• Play across both sides: drag objects, trace routes, flick comets and tap rhythms.
• Smoother folding and rotation. Compatible games rearrange; rounds that need their original layout pause and keep your answers. Resume when you're ready, or continue with a new challenge without a penalty.
• Fixes for crashes and layout issues when unfolding or rotating during a scan.
